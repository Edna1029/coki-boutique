 PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

# Store Management System

**Version:** 1.0
**Platform:** Android Mobile Application
**Framework:** Flutter
**Industry:** Clothing & Accessories Retail
**Target Users:** Store Owners, Administrators, Employees

---

# 1. Project Overview

## Purpose

Store Management System is a production-ready Flutter mobile application designed for clothing and accessories businesses.

The system enables:

* Inventory Management
* Product Variant Management
* Sales Processing
* Credit Sales Tracking
* Customer Payment Tracking
* Employee Management
* Reporting & Analytics
* Barcode & QR Scanning
* PDF Receipt Generation
* Excel Exporting
* Offline Operation
* Cloud Synchronization
* English & Amharic Support

The application must work seamlessly both online and offline while maintaining data integrity and synchronization between local and cloud databases.

---

# 2. Business Goals

The application should:

* Reduce manual inventory tracking
* Prevent stock inconsistencies
* Improve sales management
* Track customer credit balances
* Provide business insights through reports
* Support Ethiopian businesses through bilingual operation
* Function without internet access
* Scale from small stores to large retail operations

---

# 11. Inventory Rules

When adding stock:

If:

* Product Name
* Brand
* Color
* Gender
* Size

match an existing product variant,

then:

Increase quantity instead of creating a duplicate product.

Example:

Before:

```text
Blue Nike Shirt
Medium
Quantity = 12
```

New Shipment:

```text
Quantity = 5
```

After:

```text
Quantity = 17
```
